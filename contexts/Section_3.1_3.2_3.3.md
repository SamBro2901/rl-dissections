The Section 4.1 context (Part 11 item 3) already notes that `gpu_control`/`thermal_gate`/CodeCarbon loggers do not write to `run.log`, so the grep cannot detect those failures; `metadata.json` records the lock only as requested.
5. `CLAUDE.md` says the correlation snapshot has "27 configs on HalfCheetah-v5, 29 on Ant-v5": **confirmed** (7+7+8+5 and 7+9+8+5).
