\chapter{Full Result Tables}
\label{app:full-results}

This appendix gives the results of \zcref{ch:per-algorithm} and \zcref{ch:comparison} at full precision and in the full extent that the main text refers to.
Nothing is added to the interpretation.
\Zcref{sec:app-full-ch4} contains the tables of the four sections of \zcref{ch:per-algorithm} for all configurations, \zcref{sec:app-full-ec} the statistics behind \zcref{sec:energy-vs-compute}, \zcref{sec:app-full-rob} the orderings behind \zcref{sec:robustness}, and \zcref{sec:app-full-cross} the cross-seed table of all \num{56} configurations.
\Zcref{sec:app-full-opt} holds optional blocks that wait for a decision of the author.
All tables are generated from the raw run files and the context files of the repository by \texttt{flop\_analysis/appendix\_tables.py}.

\paragraph{Conventions.} As in \zcref{ch:per-algorithm}, a value is the mean over the five canonical seeds and the number after the plus--minus sign is the sample standard deviation ($n-1$); means are given with six and spreads with four significant digits.
All energies are gross (\zcref{sec:units}).
J/FLOP is the ratio of the mean energy to the mean FLOPs, and its spread is the standard deviation of the five per-seed ratios.
Shares and percentages are computed per run and then averaged.
The segment names are abbreviated in the tables; the abbreviations are listed in each caption.

\subsection{Agreement with the Main Text}\label{sec:app-full-verify}

\Zcref{tab:app-verification} reports the comparison of the tables of this appendix with the tables printed in \zcref{ch:per-algorithm} and \zcref{ch:comparison}.
For each table of the main text, every printed number was looked up among the recomputed values and compared at the number of digits printed with it; the numbers that are not tabulated in this appendix (ratios between the environments and similar derived columns) were not looked up and are counted as not found.
In addition, the cells of the \num{15} baseline tables of \zcref{ch:per-algorithm} (energies and shares, J/FLOP, durations and power, composition) were compared row by row with the recomputed value of the same environment, segment and quantity: \num{844} of \num{846} cells equal the recomputed value rounded to the printed digits, and the other two differ in the last printed digit (the standard deviation of the energy of \texttt{target\_update} of TD-MPC2 on HalfCheetah-v5, printed \num{109.8}, recomputed \num{109.7498}; the standard deviation of the RAM share in the gradient updates of TD-MPC2 on HalfCheetah-v5, printed \num{0.1613}, recomputed \num{0.16125}, a rounding tie).
